03 / Design tradeoffs

What would I test with players?

Early game motivation

Can a new player afford the first upgrade quickly enough to understand the reward loop? Watch where first time players leave.

Late game pacing

Does the next upgrade feel achievable without becoming automatic? Compare session length and time between purchases.

Rebirth decisions

Does resetting progression feel worth the permanent bonus? Measure how many eligible players choose to rebirth.

Economy health

Are players accumulating currency without meaningful spending choices? Track earning and spending patterns before changing prices.

This simple model assumes a fixed reward per run at each level, no starting savings, no random rewards, no ability purchases, and no player skill variance. Actual tuning would require playtesting and telemetry.
